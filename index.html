Why Wife's Bucket List

Every household runs on the same broken system: someone shouts down the hall, someone else sends a photo of a scrap of paper, and three people forget the milk anyway. A note you screenshot isn't a shared list.

One shared list, live

Everyone in the household adds to the same list, and changes appear on every phone straight away.

Sorted by aisle

Every item lands under its category — produce, dairy, bakery — so you walk the shop once. Type it in English or Roman Urdu.

Knows what's running low

Wife's Bucket List learns how often your household buys things and suggests them when they're due. One tap puts them back on the list.

Regulars add themselves

With Premium, turn on auto-add and the things you buy on a steady rhythm come back on the list by themselves.

Tick off together

Whoever's shopping ticks items as they go, and everyone else watches the list shrink in real time.

Know who added what

Every item shows who put it on the list, so you can ask before you guess.

Works offline

Add, tick and edit in the dead zone at the back of the shop; it syncs the moment you have signal again.
