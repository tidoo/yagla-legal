Privacy Policy — YAGLA

Last updated: 11 August 2026

YAGLA ("we", "us", "the app") is operated by Arslan Aziz, based in Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan. YAGLA is a shared household grocery list app. This document explains what data the app collects, why, who processes it, and what rights you have.

1. Data we collect

Category Source Why we collect it
Email address and name Google Sign-In, via Supabase Auth To create your account, keep you signed in, and show the other members of your household who added what
Household and list contents You and the people in your household These are the lists themselves — household name, list names, item names and quantities, who added each item, what has been ticked off, and when. Those tick times build a shopping history of up to 400 days, used only to suggest items your household buys regularly and, if you turn it on, to add them back automatically
Household membership Created when you make or join a household To share lists with the right people and no one else
Purchase history Google Play, via RevenueCat To know whether your household has an active subscription and unlock premium features
Push notification token Firebase Cloud Messaging To tell you when someone adds an item or starts shopping
App usage events PostHog, Firebase Analytics To understand which features are used, so we can improve the app. You can turn this off
Approximate location Derived from your IP address by PostHog Coarse, country/city-level only. We never request GPS or device location permission
Device identifiers PostHog, Firebase To count devices and group events from the same install
Crash reports and diagnostics Firebase Crashlytics To find and fix crashes

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, audio, files, or any health or financial data beyond the purchase status Google Play reports to us.

2. How we use your data

  • Run your account and keep you signed in.
  • Store and sync your household's lists between the people in it, in real time.
  • Send notifications when someone adds an item or starts shopping, if you leave those switches on in Settings.
  • Determine whether your household has an active subscription.
  • Measure feature usage and diagnose crashes so the app gets better.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The services below process data on our behalf as our service providers, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) — account, household and list data. Hosted in the ap-northeast-2 region (Seoul, South Korea). https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — sign-in, push notifications, analytics and crash reporting. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) — subscription status. Receives your user ID and email address. https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • PostHog (PostHog Inc.) — product analytics, US cloud. Receives your user ID and email address, usage events, device identifiers and IP address. https://posthog.com/privacy
  • Google Play (Google LLC) — processes your payment. We never see or store your card details. https://policies.google.com/privacy

Everyone in your household can see the lists, items, member names and member email addresses of that household. That is the purpose of the app. Anyone who has your household's six-character invite code can join and see the same, so share it only with people you want in your household.

4. Your rights

You can:

  • Delete your account and all associated data in the app, from Settings → App settings → Delete account. When the last member of a household leaves or deletes their account, that household and all of its lists and items are deleted automatically.
  • Leave a household at any time from Settings → Leave household, which ends your access to its lists.
  • Turn off analytics in Settings → App settings → Privacy → Analytics. Crash reporting continues so we can keep the app stable.
  • Turn off notifications per type in Settings, or in Android system settings.
  • Request a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing yagla.app@gmail.com.

Data is fully purged within 30 days of account deletion.

5. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our services uses HTTPS.
  • Access to household data is enforced in the database itself with row-level security, so a member of one household cannot read another household's lists.
  • Any secrets stored on your device are encrypted at rest using AES-GCM with keys held in the Android Keystore.
  • We do not store payment card details at any point; Google Play handles payment.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children

YAGLA is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email yagla.app@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. International transfers and GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, note that we operate from Pakistan and our service providers store data in South Korea (Supabase) and the United States (PostHog, Firebase, RevenueCat). Your data is therefore transferred outside the EEA/UK.

Our legal bases for processing are:

  • Contract — your account, your household's lists, and your subscription status. Without these the app cannot work.
  • Consent — analytics, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings, and notifications, which you can switch off.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting and security, to keep the app working and protect it from abuse.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. Email yagla.app@gmail.com to exercise any of these. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident you have the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to request its deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not process it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Email yagla.app@gmail.com to make a request.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

10. Contact
